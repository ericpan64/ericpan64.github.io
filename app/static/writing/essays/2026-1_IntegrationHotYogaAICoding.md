# Data Integration, Hot Yoga, and AI Coding

> 2025 has been an incredibly exciting and exhausing year. As I've been nagivating my own personal life transitions, I've appreciated the moments of slowing down -- both for rest and confronting the difficult questions. This essay explores two concurrent arcs and how AI has forced a decision for both.

## Two Arcs, Seven Years

Around seven years ago, I started two journeys. The first: moving from California to Wisconsin to work at Epic Systems as a data integration engineer. The second: getting dragged to a hot yoga class by a friend who promised it "wouldn't be that hard." Well, both turned out to be hard and rewarding for different reasons.

At Epic, I learned that healthcare data integration requires both precise code and and collaboration across various organizations with different technical standards, priorities, unspoken assumptions, and needs which mostly aligned. 

* EDI TS -- figuring out root causes in workflows, data. All data told a story and traced back to decisions and people. Uncovering that story in constructive, blameless, and high accountabiltiy way. Being a professional about it.


At Hot 8 Yoga in Madison, I learned that my body had opinions that my mind had been ignoring. In a class of about 15 other people (mostly women much more fit than I was), I accepted it was too late and committed to the bit. Within fifthteen minutes of class, I was in a full body sweat, constantly breaking, and side-eyeing the clock. At the end of the sculpt class, I half-jokingly threw my foam block at my friend: "Julie... what the hell?"

## Your Body Knows Before You Do

* Deciding to sign-up for class pass, however struggling go
* At the time, saw surface-level reasons (hard, by myself, insecure. People were very nice)
  * Deeper reason: I wasn't ready, and energy was from me. Couldn't integrate into space because I hadn't integrated myself. It was the right move to go, though wasn't ready

## What Integration Actually means

* Defining integration: communicating semantics between parties to address needs
* Best-case: perfect alignment. Worst-case: miscommunicated contracts and expectations. Healthcare moves at the speed of trust
* Opposite of integration: fragmentation - unorganized needs.

## AI Coding and the Temptation to Skip Steps
* AI transforming how I write code, outsourcing a lot of the "how" of programming
    * Temptation to prioritize "productivity" over all else, focus on the outcomes rather than the process
* Outsourcing care removes craft. Craft develops taste.
* Temptation with AI to skips steps, in reality it should amplify the process and fill-in the gaps bridging understanding and implementation.
* Speed as motion as progression

## Integration as Practice
* Hot 8 yoga experience -- discovering growth
* Savasana -- the most important part of class
* Taking time to process, progress is inherently piecewise, the one-shot is truly rare and often not particularily meaningful

## Conclusion: Multiple Things Breathing Together
* Accepting the different pieces of me, confronting truths. Facing the hard thing. Trying to make it work when it feels messy
